- 911 remains the emergency route. 988 is call/text crisis support, free/confidential/24/7. HRSA confirms call/text **1-833-TLC-MAMA (1-833-852-6262)**, free/confidential/24/7. These are source checks, not placed calls. Guam carrier routing for the maternal line is untested; the app says so.
- CDC's maternal threshold **100.4°F = 38°C**, inability to drink **more than 8 hours** or eat **more than 24 hours**, postpartum bleeding **one or more pads/hour**, clots **bigger than an egg**, and arm/leg sign's **up to 6 weeks after birth** match its live details. The overall list covers pregnancy and **the year after delivery**; it is not exhaustive. These are CDC's maternal signs, not general baby thresholds.
- AAP’s young-baby fever route says **3 months or younger**, **100.4°F/38°C or higher**, immediate call even without other symptoms. Its page also uses that cutoff for mouth, ear and forehead readings; underarm fever can have a lower cutoff. Rectal is most reliable in infants, ear thermometers are not advised below 6 months, and AAP’s newborn page says no fever medicine before being seen under 12 weeks. This is not an all-clear from a lower reading or a different method.
- Term definitions: **37+0–38+6 early term; 39+0–40+6 full term; 41+0–41+6 late term; 42+0 onward postterm**. Current ACOG definition was available through its primary search text. Monitoring frequency and due-date reassurance were removed; the care team sets the plan.
