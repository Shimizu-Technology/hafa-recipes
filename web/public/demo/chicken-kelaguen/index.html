Chicken Kelaguen

Serves 4 · 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb boneless chicken thighs
  • ⅓ cup lemon juice
  • ¾ cup grated fresh coconut
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • 1 red chile, chopped
  • ½ tsp sea salt

Steps

  1. Grill the chicken until cooked through, then chop it finely.
  2. Toss with the lemon juice and salt.
  3. Mix in the coconut, green onions and chile. Taste and serve.
